About this directory

A useful starting point
for the community.

Maintained by Ian McCulloch. This calendar covers tensor-network methods and selected related meetings in entanglement, quantum information and many-body physics, with broader regional coverage in Asia.

Entries link to organizer or institutional sources. Open an entry’s details to see its recorded deadlines and last check. The list is selective; a missing deadline does not mean registration is open. Longer research programmes are labelled separately from short meetings.

Suggestions and corrections are welcome through the repository.
